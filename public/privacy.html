PinBuddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

PinBuddy is a personal tool for preparing and publishing Pins. It helps the account owner write a title and description for their own photos and videos and publish them to their own Pinterest account.

What stays on your device

The photos and videos you select, the generated titles and descriptions, and your settings are stored only in your browser on your device (IndexedDB and local storage). PinBuddy has no user database and does not keep copies of your media on a server.

What is sent to Anthropic

To write a caption, PinBuddy sends a downscaled image (or up to three frames of a video) to the Anthropic Claude API using the API key you provide. Anthropic processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy.

What is sent to Pinterest

When you connect your Pinterest account, you sign in on Pinterest's own page (OAuth). PinBuddy requests access only to read your boards and to create Pins. When you press “Publish”, the selected media, title, description, link, board and alt text are sent to the Pinterest API to create the Pin on your account. PinBuddy never asks for or stores your Pinterest password.

The Pinterest access token is kept on your device. A small server function is used only to exchange the sign-in code for a token and to relay your publish requests to the Pinterest API; it does not log or store tokens or content.

Disconnecting and deleting data

You can disconnect Pinterest in PinBuddy's settings, which deletes the token from your device. You can also revoke access at any time in your Pinterest account settings under connected apps. Clearing the app's data in your browser removes everything PinBuddy stored on your device.
